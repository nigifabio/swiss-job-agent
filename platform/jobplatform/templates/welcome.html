Your private assistant for a job search in Switzerland

It scans Swiss job boards and company career pages for you, ranks every posting against your skills, keeps track of your applications, and writes a tailored CV and cover letter for each job. Built for people registered with an ORP / RAV office: the monthly proof-of-search report is one click.

Find

jobs.ch, jobup.ch, Job-Room (work.swiss), company career sites and remote boards, scanned twice a day. The same job from several sites appears once.

Rank

A 0-100 match score from your own skills. Mark skills you have, or words you want to avoid, straight from a posting.

Track

New, shortlisted, applied, interview, offer: with contacts, notes and follow-up reminders.

Write

A CV and a Swiss-format cover letter per job, in the posting's language, using only facts from your own profile. Nothing is invented.

Report

The ORP / RAV "preuves de recherches" for a week or a month as PDF or Excel, and your numbers against the monthly target.

How to start

  1. Open your invite link and sign in with your Google account, or with a code sent to your e-mail. No new password.
  2. Upload your CV (PDF or Word) and/or your LinkedIn profile. Check what was read and correct it.
  3. Choose what you're looking for: roles, town, how far you'd commute, languages. Your search is built from that.
  4. Read your first results a few minutes later, and generate your first CV and letter.

Private

Each person gets a separate workspace with its own database. Other users can't see your data, and the administrator has no page to look into it.

Yours

Download everything, or delete your workspace, at any time from the Account page.

No AI service, no tracking

Your CV is never sent to an AI or to a third party. The only outside requests go to the job sites being searched.

You apply, not a robot

It never logs into your accounts and never sends an application for you.
