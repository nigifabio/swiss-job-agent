Welcome! Let's set up your job search

Upload what you have: your CV, your LinkedIn profile, or both. We read them here, inside your own private space, to prefill your profile. You'll review everything in the next step. Nothing is sent anywhere.

On your LinkedIn profile page: More (or Resources) → Save to PDF.

How to get the export

LinkedIn → Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → choose Profile, Positions, Education, Skills, Languages, Certifications → Request archive. LinkedIn emails you a link within minutes; upload the .zip here. It is the most complete source (all positions with dates, skills, languages).
