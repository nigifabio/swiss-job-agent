My profiles

Looking for more than one kind of job, say a draughtsperson, an interior designer and a receptionist? Add a profile for each, up to {{ limit }} in all with your main one. A profile shows the same facts as your main profile, nothing is invented: it only chooses which skills, jobs, points and lines to show and in which order, with its own title line and summary. It also widens the search with its own search terms, title words, skills and words to avoid. Place, languages, work rate, exclusions and sources stay the same for all profiles.

A job that fits several profiles carries all their tags; the one where it scores highest is its main tag, and the CV and the letter of that job are written with that profile.
